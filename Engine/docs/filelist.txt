Epoch active inventory
======================

Public headers
- `Engine/include/aengine.hpp`
- `Engine/include/aengine.config.hpp`
- `Engine/include/epoch.config.hpp`
- supporting API/common headers under `Engine/include/`

Primary module families
- `aengine.*` core runtime, platform, logger, updater, bindings, systems
- `acontext.*` backend/context integrations
- `aecs.*` and `aentity.*` ECS surfaces
- `aatlas.*`, sprite, texture, and GUI support
- `epoch.*` higher-level engine/runtime modules

Examples and tooling
- `Engine/examples/ConsoleApplication1/`
- `Engine/build.sh`, `Engine/run.sh`, `Engine/install.sh`, `Engine/clean.sh`

Archive
- `Engine/legacy/include/` compatibility headers
- `Engine/legacy/src/` older runtime/source snapshots
